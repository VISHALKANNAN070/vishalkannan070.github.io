001. My Mind Became Zero

29 May 2026

I wanted to start writing blogs on my website just like other people who write useful blogs.

But I don't have that level of knowledge and experience. So I decided to write whatever comes to my mind.

But when I decided to write and opened a text editor, my mind became blank like an A4 sheet.

It felt different because I spend so much time watching YouTube, listening to music, arguing with friends, doing something.

But when I wanted to be quiet and bring my focus on writing something, I couldn't do that.

Why is it like this?

Maybe I forgot how to exist without getting bored. And when I try to force it, my brain does not know what to do with the silence.

It constantly wants to consume something.

Why should I allow that?

But ironically, this led me to writing this as a post.

I don't think this is professional, but I will be at peace knowing that I wrote something on my own, on my own website. No one's questioning it.

So this is my first blog post.

PS: I'm reading a book now and I will make a review on it in the future. The title of the book is "The Theory of Everything" by Stephen Hawking.
